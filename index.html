Product direction

A focused path into real-time conversation assistance.

Our current product concept is KaiwaKit, a browser-based assistant designed to help users understand spoken foreign-language conversations and prepare concise, natural replies.

KaiwaKit concept

Listen to the conversation, translate the meaning, and suggest what to say next.

KaiwaKit is being shaped around live speech recognition, fast translation, and AI-generated reply options for everyday situations where a user needs language help in the moment.

Browser-first direction

The product direction favors web access, low setup cost, and a compact interface that can be evaluated quickly.

Human-centered output

Operating principles

Built for practical review, controlled scope, and responsible integration.

We keep the public footprint straightforward: real company information, specific product intent, plain-language policies, and clear contact paths.

01

Focused product surface

We prefer narrow products with a concrete job over broad platforms that dilute execution and review clarity.

02

Provider-agnostic design

AI, speech, and translation providers are treated as replaceable layers so quality, latency, and cost can be evaluated honestly.

03

Data-minimal UX

Product decisions are oriented around showing users what they need now while avoiding unnecessary collection or account complexity.

04

Measurable usefulness

The product concept prioritizes response speed, recognition quality, and practical language output that can be tested in realistic scenarios.
